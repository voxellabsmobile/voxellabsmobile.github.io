General Legal Disclaimer & Intellectual Property Notice

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Published by VoxelLabs Mobile • Universal Policy Applicable to All Present & Future Mobile Titles

This General Legal Disclaimer and Intellectual Property Notice sets forth the official legal posture, nominative fair use doctrine, open-standards compliance, and notice-and-takedown procedures for all mobile applications, games, software, and digital media published by VoxelLabs Mobile ("VoxelLabs Mobile", "we", "our", or "us") on the Google Play Store and other authorized distribution channels (collectively, our "Apps").

Summary of Developer Legal Posture: VoxelLabs Mobile creates original casual, puzzle, trivia, arcade, and entertainment software. We operate with strict respect for legitimate intellectual property rights while maintaining an unyielding legal defense against trademark trolling, opportunistic extortion, and bad-faith infringement claims targeting independent game developers.

1. Nominative Fair Use & Cultural Trivia / Quiz Disclaimer

Many of our mobile games (including puzzle, trivia, word guessing, and brain teaser games) test players' cultural knowledge through riddles, associative clues, and trivia questions. Puzzles or questions may reference common idioms, famous literature, movie titles, songs, sports franchises, historical events, geographic landmarks, or popular culture figures.

Under international trademark jurisprudence and Section 33(b)(4) of the United States Lanham Act (15 U.S.C. § 1115(b)(4)), such references constitute Nominative Fair Use:

  • Sole Purpose of Identification: The use of any title, name, or cultural term is strictly necessary to identify the subject matter of the trivia riddle or question. There is no other practical linguistic method to refer to the riddle's answer without using that name.
  • No Greater Use Than Necessary: Our games do not reproduce official proprietary corporate logos, registered stylized logotypes, or protected commercial trade dress. Clues and answers are displayed solely using standard text typography or universal open emojis.
  • No Suggestion of Endorsement: Nothing in our applications suggests, implies, or intends to imply any sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or affiliation with any third-party franchise, studio, celebrity, or trademark proprietor.

2. Game Mechanics & The Idea-Expression Dichotomy

In accordance with well-established international copyright conventions and 17 U.S.C. § 102(b), copyright protection does not extend to ideas, procedures, processes, systems, methods of operation, concepts, mathematical principles, or game mechanics (e.g., matching tiles, tapping mechanics, trivia quiz structures, drag-and-drop interactions, endless progression, or scoring rules).

All source code, user interface layouts, visual artwork, animations, sound design, and specific implementations in VoxelLabs Mobile games represent original expressive authorship independently crafted by VoxelLabs Mobile.

3. Universal Open Standards, Emojis & Open-Source Assets

Our mobile titles may incorporate open-standard graphical characters and open-source resources:

  • Unicode Emojis: Emojis displayed in our games are standardized characters established by the Unicode Consortium as universal digital glyphs. They are rendered natively by the player's Android operating system or sourced from open-source font packages under permissive licenses (such as SIL Open Font License, Apache 2.0, or CC-BY). Emojis are public linguistic utilities; no third party possesses exclusive trademark monopoly over standard Unicode characters.
  • Open-Source Fonts & Audio: All fonts, sound effects, and graphical libraries utilized across our games are either custom-created or licensed under verified royalty-free, commercial-friendly licenses.

All trademarks, service marks, trade names, product names, and brand logos mentioned or referenced within any of our games are the property of their respective legal owners.

VoxelLabs Mobile is an independent indie mobile game studio. Reference to any third party, commercial product, television show, motion picture, or recording artist does not imply or constitute an endorsement, authorization, or sponsorship by that entity.

5. Policy Against Bad-Faith Claims & Trademark Trolling

VoxelLabs Mobile explicitly recognizes the growing predatory practice known as "trademark trolling"—where opportunistic actors register common, descriptive, generic, or dictionary words (e.g., "Puzzle", "Trivia", "Emoji", "Hero", "Runner", "Match", "Tap"), or file automated, unsubstantiated copyright strikes against indie developers in an attempt to extort unwarranted financial settlements or licensing fees.

Strict Policy on Extortionate Financial Demands:
VoxelLabs Mobile maintains a strict, zero-ransom policy. We do not pay settlements, licensing ransoms, or extortionate fees to parties asserting generic, descriptive, or bad-faith claims. Any party attempting to extort VoxelLabs Mobile without verifiable registered trademark ownership in relevant Nice Classes (specifically Class 9 for downloadable software or Class 41 for game entertainment) will be:
  • Immediately reported to Google Play Legal Support and relevant governmental authorities (including the FTC and cybercrime units) for fraudulent claims and abuse of process.
  • Subject to aggressive legal counterclaims for tortious interference with business relations, lost app revenue, and full recovery of statutory legal defense fees.

6. Good-Faith Notice and Takedown Procedure (Safe Harbor)

If you are a verified rights holder (or an authorized legal representative) and genuinely believe in good faith that specific content in any VoxelLabs Mobile application infringes upon your registered trademark or copyright, we are fully committed to resolving legitimate concerns swiftly, amicably, and respectfully without the need for litigation.

To file a valid intellectual property inquiry, please transmit an official written notice to our designated legal communications desk at voxellabsmobile@gmail.com containing:

  1. Proof of Ownership: Registration certificate, registration number(s), jurisdiction, and Nice Class classification of the registered trademark or copyright claimed to be infringed.
  2. Exact Content Location: The title of the specific VoxelLabs Mobile game, the specific level/screen/element, and a screenshot identifying the disputed material.
  3. Claimant Information: Your full legal name, business name, physical address, telephone number, and official corporate email address.
  4. Good-Faith Statement: A formal statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the intellectual property owner, its agent, or the law (including Nominative Fair Use).
  5. Statement Under Penalty of Perjury: A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  6. Authorized Signature: An electronic or physical signature of the authorized copyright/trademark representative.

Developer Safe-Harbor Commitment: Upon receipt of a complete and legitimate notice, VoxelLabs Mobile will review the claim and, where appropriate, modify, update, or remove the specific disputed in-game content within 48 to 72 business hours, without admission of liability.
