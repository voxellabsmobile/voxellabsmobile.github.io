General Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Published by VoxelLabs Mobile • Universal Policy for All Present & Future Mobile Applications

At VoxelLabs Mobile ("VoxelLabs Mobile", "we", "our", or "us"), we develop and publish casual, trivia, puzzle, arcade, and entertainment mobile applications for players worldwide. This Privacy Policy applies comprehensively to all mobile applications, games, software, and digital services published under the VoxelLabs Mobile developer account across Google Play Store and other authorized mobile application platforms (collectively referred to as our "Apps" or "Services").

This document details our strict adherence to Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google Play Data Safety guidelines, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Key Privacy Principle: We prioritize user privacy and data minimization. Our games do NOT require mandatory account registration with personal credentials, do NOT collect your legal name, physical address, phone number, contacts list, microphone audio, camera feeds, or precise GPS location. Our games can be played anonymously and safely.

1. Google Play Data Safety Disclosures (Data We Process)

To maintain transparency across all our mobile titles in compliance with Google Play Data Safety standards, the following table details the categories of data that may be processed across our applications:

Data Category Data Elements Purpose Storage & Handling
Gameplay & App Activity Game progress, level completions, puzzle answers, high scores, virtual coins, stars, or unlockable items Core game functionality, progress saving, and player progression Stored locally on your device storage (SharedPreferences / SQLite / Room). Never sold.
Public Profiles & Leaderboards (Optional) Randomly generated anonymous player ID, chosen display nickname, chosen avatar icon/flag, game score/stars Competitive public online leaderboards and global rankings Synchronized securely to cloud databases (such as Google Cloud Firestore). Contains no real-world identity.
Diagnostics & Technical Performance Crash logs, stack traces, Application Not Responding (ANR) reports, device model, Android OS version Analytics, bug fixing, stability optimization, and crash prevention Collected through Google Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics. Transmitted via encrypted HTTPS.
Advertising Identifiers & Ad Metrics Google Advertising ID (GAID), ad impressions, click events, ad format views Delivering contextual, rewarded, banner, or interstitial advertisements and mediation yield optimization Processed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob Next-Gen SDK) and certified mediation partners (AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Vungle/Liftoff, Unity Ads). Configurable via Android device settings.
Purchases & Virtual Items (If Applicable) Purchase order token, item SKU identifier, timestamp Fulfilling in-app purchases (e.g. ad removal, extra hints, item packs) Processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. VoxelLabs Mobile never receives or stores payment card numbers.

3. Children's Online Privacy & Family Policy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

VoxelLabs Mobile is deeply dedicated to child safety and family-friendly mobile entertainment:

  • Our applications are designed for general audiences and family entertainment.
  • We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • In titles directed toward families or where children may be part of the audience, our ad mediation system automatically passes COPPA tags (such as tagForChildDirectedTreatment and isAgeRestrictedUser) to Google AdMob, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, and Vungle, ensuring that third-party ad networks serve strictly contextual, non-targeted, and family-appropriate advertisements.
  • If a parent or legal guardian believes that an underage player has submitted any personal data (such as a public leaderboard nickname), they may request prompt removal by emailing us at voxellabsmobile@gmail.com.

4. User Data Deletion & Retention Policy (Mandatory Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's mandatory Account and Data Deletion policies, we provide transparent, immediate, and straightforward mechanisms for players to erase their data across any of our applications:

  • Local Data Deletion (Direct on Device): All locally saved progress, unlocked stages, scores, and cached virtual currency can be deleted at any moment without contacting us. Simply open your Android device settings:
    Settings > Apps > [Select Any VoxelLabs Mobile Game] > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage
    Alternatively, uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local application sandboxed data.
  • Online Cloud Leaderboard Data Deletion: If you submitted a score or display name to any online leaderboard across our games and wish to exercise your legal "Right to be Forgotten", you can submit a deletion request via email:
    Email: voxellabsmobile@gmail.com
    Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Game / App Name]
    Required Details: Name of the game, your in-game nickname, and approximate score/rank so our system administrator can locate and purge the specific record.
    All verified cloud data deletion requests are processed completely free of charge within 7 business days.

5. Advertising Controls & Opt-Out Options

Android users have complete native control over advertising personalization. You can manage or disable interest-based ads at any time via your device settings:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID: On Android 12 and above, go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID to disassociate ad metrics from your device entirely.

6. Data Security

We employ robust, industry-standard administrative and technical security measures. All communications between our applications and third-party backend endpoints (e.g., Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play) are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ protocols.
